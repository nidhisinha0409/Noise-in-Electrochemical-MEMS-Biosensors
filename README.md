# Noise in Electrochemical MEMS Biosensors

## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive review of noise mechanisms, characterization techniques, and mitigation strategies in electrochemical MEMS biosensors.

The work explores how electronic, electrochemical, dielectric, environmental, and packaging-related noise sources affect biosensor performance and proposes advanced solutions involving material engineering, CMOS circuit design, signal processing, microfluidics, and AI-based denoising. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## Objectives

- Study noise mechanisms in electrochemical MEMS biosensors
- Analyze the impact of noise on sensor performance
- Investigate noise characterization techniques
- Evaluate hardware and software noise mitigation methods
- Explore future directions in AI-assisted biosensing

- Dielectric Relaxation
- Polymer Swelling
- Surface Charge Instability

### Readout Circuit Noise
- CMOS Flicker Noise
- ADC Quantization Noise
- TIA Thermal Noise

### Environmental Noise
- Electromagnetic Interference (EMI)
- Mechanical Vibrations
- Packaging Effects

## Impact of Noise on Biosensor Performance

Noise directly affects:

- Limit of Detection (LOD)
- Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)
- Baseline Stability
- Impedance Measurement Accuracy
- Selectivity
- Response Time

These performance parameters determine biosensor sensitivity and reliability. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
